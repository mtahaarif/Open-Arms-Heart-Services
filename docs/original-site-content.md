### Non-Skilled Home Health Services
"Open Arms & Heart Services, Inc. is a Florida-licensed home health agency offering non-medical support to children, adults, seniors, and people with disabilities. Our caregivers help individuals remain comfortable and supported in their homes."
Services and Support: Personal Care, bathing, dressing, grooming, and hygiene assistance · Mobility and transfer assistance · Homemaker services, light housekeeping, and meal preparation · Companionship, socialization, and respite care · Assistance with activities of daily living · Pediatric personal care and support for people with disabilities
"Our caregivers complete applicable background screening and role-appropriate training. Service areas: Polk, Highlands, Hardee, Hillsborough, and Manatee Counties."

### Agency for Persons with Disabilities (APD)
"As an APD provider, Open Arms offers person-centered supports designed to strengthen daily living skills, increase independence, and encourage community inclusion."
Services and Support: Personal Supports · Life Skills Development Level 1 (Companion) · Supported Living Coach · Supported Employment · Residential Habitation (Group Home) *(typo for "Habilitation")* · Respite Care · Community integration and daily living skill development
"We collaborate with individuals, families, and Waiver Support Coordinators to support each person's goals and support plan. APD Central Region counties: Orange, Brevard, Sumter, Lake, Polk, and Highlands."

### Vulnerable Adult Support Services/DCF Referrals
"Open Arms supports adults who need assistance maintaining dignity, safety, and independence. We may coordinate appropriate services with families, social workers, case managers, and community referral partners."
Services and Support: Personal care and homemaker assistance · Companion services and social support · Activities of daily living assistance · Caregiver respite · Support for older adults and adults with disabilities · Referrals and coordination for eligible home-based support
"Open Arms does not conduct DCF Adult Protective Services investigations or represent itself as the state protective services agency. Services depend on eligibility, authorization, and agency capacity."

### Children & Family Support Services (CBC)
"Open Arms has experience working with community-based care organizations and foster families to provide supportive, compassionate services. Current availability depends on active referral and contracting arrangements."
Services and Support: Foster care respite experience · Caregiver relief and family support · Support for children with special needs or developmental disabilities · Individualized personal care assistance · Community-based support and supervision · Coordination with case managers and child welfare professionals
"We welcome conversations with CBC organizations and community partners about appropriate service opportunities."

### Veterans Home Care & Respite Services (VA)
"Open Arms is proud to support eligible veterans and their families through respectful, individualized home and community-based assistance, subject to applicable VA Community Care referral and authorization requirements."
Services and Support: Personal care and homemaker assistance · Companion services and social support · Activities of daily living assistance · Caregiver respite · Support for older adults and adults with disabilities · Referrals and coordination for eligible home-based support
"Veterans and family members may contact Open Arms to discuss available services and required referrals or authorizations."

### Live Scan Fingerprinting & Background Screening
"Open Arms offers Live Scan fingerprinting services to assist with applicable Florida background screening and licensing requirements."
Services and Support: Electronic Live Scan fingerprint capture · Florida Level 2 screening submissions, where authorized · Healtchare *(sic)*, childcare, education, and professional licensing fingerprinting requests, where supported · Employer and organizational screening requests, where supported
"Contact our office for appointments, fees, identification requirements, and confirmation that we can process your specific agency or ORI submission."
