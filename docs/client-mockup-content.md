## Client feedback (2026-10-24)

> The current design feels a little too busy for my taste … Could we review the Mind & Mobility website along with Ms. Lynn's website for inspiration? I would like a cleaner, more polished design with easy navigation, a more professional font, and welcoming pictures that reflect the people we serve. Look at the link I put together via ChatGPT, although I would like to change the pictures a little and the font, but it's more of my idea of my site.

How it was applied:
- **Cleaner layout:** the mock-up's section structure, colours and plain layout.
- **More professional font:** Source Serif 4 for headings and Source Sans 3 for body text, replacing the mock-up's Manrope and the previous Fraunces, Figtree and script fonts.
- **Welcoming pictures:** new photos of the people the agency serves (seniors, veterans, adults with disabilities, children and families) and of diverse caregivers.
- **Busy effects removed:** no marquees, stacking or pinned scroll effects, glow, tilt, glass, smooth-scroll hijacking or page-transition curtains.
